## 2. Books & Publications

### Essential Books (Must Read)

#### **Python & Programming**
1. **"Fluent Python" (3rd Edition, 2025)** by Luciano Ramalho
   - Deep dive into Python
   - Advanced features and best practices
   - ~1000 pages
   
2. **"Effective Python" (3rd Edition, 2024)** by Brett Slatkin
   - 90+ best practices
   - Code optimization
   - ~400 pages

#### **Data Structures & Algorithms**
3. **"Introduction to Algorithms" (4th Edition, 2022)** by CLRS
   - Comprehensive algorithm reference
   - Mathematical rigor
   - ~1300 pages
   
4. **"Grokking Algorithms"** by Aditya Bhargava
   - Visual, intuitive explanations
   - Great for beginners
   - ~250 pages

#### **Machine Learning**
5. **"Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and TensorFlow" (3rd Edition, 2025)** by Aurélien Géron
   - **BEST practical ML book**
   - Covers basics to advanced
   - Includes MLOps
   - ~900 pages
   
6. **"The Hundred-Page Machine Learning Book"** by Andriy Burkov
   - Concise ML overview
   - Quick reference
   - ~100 pages (as promised!)

7. **"Machine Learning Engineering"** by Andriy Burkov
   - Production ML systems
   - Real-world challenges
   - ~300 pages

#### **Deep Learning**
8. **"Deep Learning"** by Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville
   - **The** deep learning textbook
   - Mathematical foundations
   - ~800 pages
   
9. **"Deep Learning with Python" (2nd Edition, 2024)** by François Chollet
   - Practical deep learning
   - Keras creator's insights
   - ~500 pages

#### **Specialized Topics**
10. **"Natural Language Processing with Transformers" (2nd Edition, 2025)** by Tunstall, von Werra, Wolf
    - Hugging Face team
    - Comprehensive NLP
    - ~400 pages

11. **"Speech and Language Processing" (3rd Edition, 2024)** by Jurafsky & Martin
    - NLP foundations
    - FREE online
    - ~600 pages

12. **"Designing Data-Intensive Applications"** by Martin Kleppmann
    - **Essential for production systems**
    - Scalability, reliability
    - ~600 pages

13. **"Building LLM Applications for Production" (2025)** by Valentina Alto
    - 2026 relevant
    - RAG, agents, evaluation
    - ~350 pages

#### **MLOps & Production**
14. **"Introducing MLOps"** by Treveil et al.
    - End-to-end ML systems
    - Best practices
    - ~400 pages

15. **"Machine Learning Systems Design"** by Chip Huyen
    - System design for ML
    - Interviews preparation
    - ~300 pages

#### **Mathematics**
16. **"Mathematics for Machine Learning"** by Deisenroth, Faisal, Ong
    - Linear algebra, calculus, probability
    - FREE PDF available
    - ~400 pages

17. **"An Introduction to Statistical Learning" (2nd Edition, 2023)** by James et al.
    - Statistical methods
    - R implementations
    - FREE PDF available
    - ~600 pages

#### **System Design**
18. **"System Design Interview Volume 1 & 2"** by Alex Xu
    - Scalable systems
    - Visual explanations
    - ~300 pages each

## 7. Research Papers & Preprints

### Paper Repositories

#### **arXiv.org**
- **Most important**: cs.LG, cs.AI, cs.CV, cs.CL
- Daily paper releases
- No peer review (preprints)
- **How to use**: Subscribe to daily digests

#### **Papers with Code**
- Papers + implementation code
- Benchmarks and leaderboards
- SOTA tracking
- **URL**: paperswithcode.com

#### **Semantic Scholar**
- AI-powered paper search
- Citation analysis
- Paper recommendations

#### **Google Scholar**
- Comprehensive search
- Citation tracking
- Author profiles

### Must-Read Papers (Foundational)

#### **Deep Learning**
1. "ImageNet Classification with Deep CNNs" (AlexNet, 2012)
2. "Very Deep Convolutional Networks" (VGG, 2014)
3. "Deep Residual Learning" (ResNet, 2015)
4. "Batch Normalization" (2015)
5. "Dropout: A Simple Way to Prevent Overfitting" (2014)

#### **Transformers & NLP**
6. "Attention Is All You Need" (Transformer, 2017) - **MUST READ**
7. "BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers" (2018)
8. "Language Models are Few-Shot Learners" (GPT-3, 2020)
9. "LLaMA: Open and Efficient Foundation Language Models" (2023)

#### **Computer Vision**
10. "U-Net: Convolutional Networks for Biomedical Image Segmentation" (2015)
11. "You Only Look Once: Unified, Real-Time Object Detection" (YOLO, 2015)
12. "An Image is Worth 16x16 Words" (Vision Transformer, 2020)

#### **Generative AI**
13. "Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models" (2020)
14. "High-Resolution Image Synthesis with Latent Diffusion Models" (Stable Diffusion, 2021)

#### **Reinforcement Learning**
15. "Playing Atari with Deep Reinforcement Learning" (DQN, 2013)
16. "Proximal Policy Optimization" (PPO, 2017)

### Paper Reading Strategy
1. **Abstract**: Understand main contribution
2. **Introduction**: Context and motivation
3. **Figures**: Visual understanding
4. **Conclusion**: Results summary
5. **Method** (if needed): Technical details
6. **Experiments** (if relevant): Validation

**Time**: 20-30 minutes per paper initially, 5-10 minutes later
